Process

Three steps to your benefit.

From a simple conversation to a submitted government application — entirely hands-free.

01

Tell us about yourself

Just text the bot your basic details in plain English or Hindi. No forms, no portals, no jargon.

02

Secure your documents

Upload a photo of your Aadhaar or other documents. Our local Vision AI extracts and encrypts your data — it never leaves your machine.

03

AI fills the application

Yojana Mitra navigates the government portal, fills every field, and pauses for your final CONFIRM before submitting.
